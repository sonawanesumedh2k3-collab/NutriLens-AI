Nutritional Healthy Predictor Project -

Q) How have you decided the health score in your project?
The health score was derived from nutrient-based scoring using per - 100g nutritional values, with FSSAI's Indian Nutrition Rating framework used as the reference.

The official FSSAI material describes the INR calculation as:

Final INR score = baseline points − positive nutrient points.

Q) What does the apply() do?
- df_clean["calorie_points"] = df_clean["calories_per_100g"].apply(calorie_points)
Takes every values in calories_per_100g, send it through our calorie_points() function, and stores it in the calories_points column.
